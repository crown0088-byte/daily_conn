Who We Are

Daily Connect is a modern marketplace designed to formalize the unorganized sector of daily wage labor. We believe finding reliable help shouldn't rely on word-of-mouth or standing at labor chowks.

Our platform brings skilled workers like plumbers, electricians, maids, and helpers directly to your fingertips, creating fair pricing, transparency, and dignity for independent workers.

To provide a reliable, transparent, and user-friendly platform for daily wage workers, bypassing high commission agencies and empowering direct connections that respect the value of labor.

Our Vision

To become the global standard platform where independent skilled labor meets fair opportunity, reshaping local economies, ensuring digital inclusion, and bringing dignity to daily wage work.
